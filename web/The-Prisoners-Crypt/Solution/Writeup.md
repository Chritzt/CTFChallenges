## The-Prisoners-Crypt

This Challenge is a mix of popular Game Theory, the Prisoners Dilemma, and web Localstorage manipulation.


You have two options to choose, however the game is programmed in a way that you never leave with 0 years of prison, but that is the goal.

Two win the game you need to change in round 5 the oponends next move which is saved in you local storage to split and you need to give yourself 0 years of prison, which is also done in the local storage.
